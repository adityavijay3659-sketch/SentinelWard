10. PHASE 2 — A1 ALERTS IMPLEMENTED LOCALLY

Screen alert is persisted/published before attempting Telegram backup.
One stable active alert_id per bed event. Repeated verdicts update the same alert;
higher priority upgrades it; lower priority cannot silently downgrade it.
High priority starts the ladder immediately:
1) Assigned nurse immediately.
2) Next nurse after 30 seconds in demo / 60 seconds in design-real.
3) All unit nurses and on-call doctor after another 30/60 seconds.
Recipients are cumulative in AlertMessage.to; Telegram sends only to newly added
recipients. Medium/low alerts notify the assigned nurse without a timed high ladder.

Lifecycle requirements already implemented and tested:
- On my way acknowledges, cancels all remaining timers, records response time
  and edits delivered Telegram copies.
- Manual Escalate advances exactly one step and schedules the following step
  from that moment; cannot exceed step 3.
- Patient OK records the bedside result and closes the active event/ladder.
- Patient NOT OK creates/upgrades high alert, does NOT acknowledge it; ladder
  continues until a separate acknowledgement or legitimate terminal action.
- NORMAL cannot cancel an unacknowledged alert. Acknowledgement followed by
  NORMAL closes the boundary so a future event can create a new alert.
- Repeated/stale callbacks cannot repeat actions; completed duplicates return
  stored results. Unknown/unauthorized callbacks cannot mutate state.
- Telegram failure is an action/delivery failure, not failure/closure of the
  clinical alert. Screen stays active.
- Missing waveform falls back to text/buttons. Missing roster is explicit;
  cached assignments are usable only within their validity.

Persistence includes alert/delivery/roster/callback-token/callback-receipt state,

11. PHASE 3 — M6 RUNTIME IMPLEMENTATION

M6 watches whether arterial pressure is gradually becoming inaccurate while
values still look plausible. It emits evidence to the decision layer; it cannot
diagnose, issue final clinical verdicts, acknowledge alerts or control devices.

Implemented modules include:
- m6.py: causal deterministic scorer and bounded per-episode state.
- m6_persistence.py: per-bed advisory lock, state/outbox atomic transaction,
  duplicate/restart safety and episode/version boundaries.
- m6_replay.py: VitalDB CSV/gzip and locked-v1 JSONL replay, compact waveform
  clocks, resampling/alignment and explicit gaps.
- m6_evaluation.py / m6_cli.py: patient-separated reviewed-label calibration
  and frozen held-out evaluation; artifact integrity and held-out-use ledger.
- m6_demo.py: synthetic software scenarios, not clinical truth.
- m6_realdata.py: bounded public download, receipts/checksums and original experiments.
- m6_training.py: offline physiology ridge training/comparison.
- m6_cohort.py: new independent cohort reference-availability screening.
- m6_forest.py: optional offline forest training, bounded numeric JSON weights
  and standard-library inference; no executable pickle loading.
- m6_revalidation.py: full-window replay, fixed-onset controlled perturbations,
  model selection/freeze, explicit missing coverage and pre-freeze-only resume.
- m6_research_audit.py: hashes, patient-cluster uncertainty and paired additional
  injection responses; no retraining/threshold tuning.

Runtime uses 125 Hz, causal 10-second windows every five seconds. Features:
arterial MAP, amplitude/shape, HR, simple ECG-to-PPG timing proxy; current timing
proxy is not a clinically validated PAT measurement.
Original combined path: fresh-cuff gap, robust within-patient 179-window baseline,
two-sided Page-Hinkley residual change and morphology/damping evidence.
Aligned optional M3/M5 hints can inform cause; missing/misaligned evidence means
uncertain, not an inferred attack. A score alone cannot prove intent.

Research guards currently include cuff age <=120 seconds, 10 mmHg gap signal,
pressure floor 65 mmHg, substantial pressure fall 15 mmHg and evidence threshold
0.6. These are CONFIGURED RESEARCH SETTINGS, not approved clinical standards.

Version 0.3.1 cold-start fix:
- Valid ART plus a fresh cuff disagreement can produce cuff_gap_only evidence
  before a full baseline or when HR/timing is unavailable.
- A generated 100/85 mmHg fixture gives score 0.75 and research drift.
- A generated 11 mmHg gap gives 0.55, below threshold, with insufficient_data
  and WARNING: PRESSURE GAP instead of falsely saying all measured data is absent.
- This path does not fabricate predicted MAP, Page-Hinkley or full-baseline readiness.
- Disagreeing windows never enter baseline training.
- High alarms, low cuff/ART pressure and substantial falls retain patient-review
  priority BEFORE gap-only drift evidence.
- baseline_ready, baseline_required_windows, assessment_path, actual feature/gap
  evidence and clinical_validation=pending are explicit.

Baseline requires 179 eligible 10-second windows at a five-second hop: about
15 minutes if uninterrupted. The current implementation can collect eligible
windows over longer elapsed time; do not falsely say it enforces 15 consecutive
minutes. Baseline eligibility rejects missing inputs, stale cuff, disagreement
