windows below the drift threshold; it is not sensitivity or accuracy. The

This audit shows why adding rows or lowering freshness/baseline thresholds to
raise pass counts would be misleading. Separate cuff-event and waveform/timing
readiness and a causal patient-specific gap experiment are the next research
design work; verified references and reviewed truth remain prerequisites for
clinical accuracy claims. Longer recordings alone do not resolve those gaps.

The historical pressure regression remains frozen: 4,089 usable windows from
four already inspected patients, MAP MAE 8.553879 mmHg. These repairs do not
establish a new accuracy improvement or independent held-out result. Synthetic
offset/ramp/damping responses measure controlled injections, not clinical
sensitivity or confirmed naturally occurring drift. The final paired replay
checks exact equality of historical feature/target rows and model integrity.

The original 12-patient dataset failed full baseline readiness. Longer recordings
and more patients can improve coverage research, but cannot supply missing
verified cuff events or reviewed clinical truth. Those remain required, together
with representative genuine hypotension, stable and fault cases and external
integration checks. M6 stays disabled by default, research-only, and rejected
in `design-real`.
